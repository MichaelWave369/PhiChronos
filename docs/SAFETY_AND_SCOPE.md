# Safety, scientific integrity and scope

PhiChronos is educational, computational research. It does not build hazardous experimental apparatus, manipulate powerful fields, suggest exposing humans to extreme acceleration or radiation, or claim access to a physical time machine.

Use clear statuses:
- **Measured:** direct experimental observation of the relevant physical effect.
- **Model:** theoretical consequences within an explicitly stated mathematical framework.
- **Simulated:** a model emulated on ordinary hardware; not a direct observation of its hypothetical target.
- **Unverified:** missing independent evidence, or unresolved physical feasibility.

Distinguish mathematical consistency, empirical viability and engineering practicality. Mark every plot, probability or derived quantity with assumptions. Publicly correct errors and negative results.
